# Rule 38 — Scheduling family management conference if no reply filed


Subject to rules 39 [requirements to be met before scheduling family management conference in family justice registry] and 40 [requirements to be met before scheduling family management conference in parenting education registry], if a party has filed an application about a family law matter and

**(a)** a reply has not been filed,

**(b)** based on the certificate of service, at least 30 days have passed since the application about the family law matter was served, and

**(c)** if applicable, the party has met the early resolution requirements under [rule 10 [early resolution requirements must be met before application filed]](pcfr_rule_010_early_resolution_requirements_must_be_met_before_application_filed.md),

the registry must provide that party with information about the procedure for scheduling a family management conference.
