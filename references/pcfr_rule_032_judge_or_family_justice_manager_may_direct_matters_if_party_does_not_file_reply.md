# Rule 32 — Judge or family justice manager may direct matters if party does not file reply


Despite [rule 31 [if no reply filed]](pcfr_rule_031_if_no_reply_filed.md), a judge or family justice manager may

**(a)** direct that a party who does not file a reply under [rule 28 [reply to application about family law matter]](pcfr_rule_028_reply_to_application_about_family_law_matter.md) receive notice of and attend a family management conference or another conference or hearing, and

**(b)** issue a summons in Form 31 [Summons — General].
