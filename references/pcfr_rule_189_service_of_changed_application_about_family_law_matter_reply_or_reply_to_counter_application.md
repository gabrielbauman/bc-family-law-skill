# Rule 189 — Service of changed application about family law matter, reply or reply to counter application


An application about a family law matter, a reply or a reply to a counter application that is changed under [rule 188 [changing filed application about family law matter, reply or reply to counter application]](pcfr_rule_188_changing_filed_application_about_family_law_matter_reply_or_reply_to_counter_application.md) must be served on each other party within 7 days of filing the changed application about a family law matter, reply or reply to counter application.
