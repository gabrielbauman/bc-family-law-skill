# Section 19.9 — Order revoking appointment


**(1)** On application by a party, the Supreme Court may revoke the appointment of an arbitrator if satisfied that there are justifiable doubts as to the arbitrator's independence or impartiality.


**(2)** For the purposes of subsection (1), there are justifiable doubts as to the arbitrator's independence or impartiality only if there is a real danger of bias on the part of the arbitrator in conducting the arbitration.
