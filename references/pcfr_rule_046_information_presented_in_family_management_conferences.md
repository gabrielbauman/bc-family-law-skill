# Rule 46 — Information presented in family management conferences


For the purposes of a family management conference, the judge or family justice manager may require a party to provide the following for consideration:

**(a)** information provided in an application about a family law matter, reply and reply to counter application, if any;

**(b)** evidence provided in a financial statement;

**(c)** evidence given orally on oath or affirmation;

**(d)** affidavit evidence;

**(e)** submissions.
