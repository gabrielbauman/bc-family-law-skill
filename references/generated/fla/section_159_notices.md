# Section 159 — Notices


**(1)** A notice or other record required to be given under this Division or the regulations made under [section 247 [regulations respecting child support]](section_247_regulations_respecting_child_support.md) is deemed to be received on the last date of the prescribed period.


**(2)** If a child support service is required to give a notice or other record, the notice or other record must be given in accordance with the regulations.
