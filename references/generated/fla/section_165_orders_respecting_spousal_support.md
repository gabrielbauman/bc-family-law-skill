# Section 165 — Orders respecting spousal support


**(1)** On application, the court may order a spouse to pay to a designated person the amount the court considers appropriate as spousal support after taking into consideration [section 160 [duty to provide support for entitled spouse]](section_160_duty_to_provide_support_for_entitled_spouse.md).


**(2)** An application under subsection (1) may be made

**(a)** by either spouse or both,

**(b)** on behalf of a spouse, by a designated agency under the Adult Guardianship Act after an investigation conducted under Part 3 of that Act, or

**(c)** if the right to apply for an order under this section is assigned to a minister under the Employment and Assistance Act or the Employment and Assistance for Persons with Disabilities Act, by the minister to whom the right is assigned in the name of the government or the name of the person who made the assignment.


**(3)** The court may not make an order respecting spousal support if the spouses have an agreement described in [section 164 (1) [setting aside agreements respecting spousal support]](section_164_setting_aside_agreements_respecting_spousal_support.md), unless all or part of the agreement is set aside under that section.
