# Section 186 — Orders without notice


**(1)** An application for an order under this Part may be made without notice.


**(2)** If an order is made under this Part without notice, the court, on application by the party against whom the order is made, may

**(a)** set aside the order, or

**(b)** make an order under [section 187 [changing or terminating orders respecting protection]](section_187_changing_or_terminating_orders_respecting_protection.md).
