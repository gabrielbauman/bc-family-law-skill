# Section 256 — Transition — regulations


**(1)** The Lieutenant Governor in Council may make regulations the Lieutenant Governor in Council considers necessary or advisable for the orderly transition from the former Act to this Act.


**(2)** The authority to make or amend a regulation made under subsection (1), but not the authority to repeal a regulation made under subsection (1), ends 3 years after the date on which subsection (1) comes into force.
