# Rule 10 — Early resolution requirements must be met before application filed


Before filing an application about a family law matter under Part 3 [Application About Family Law Matters], a party seeking resolution of a family law matter in an early resolution registry must, unless otherwise provided in these rules,

**(a)** file a notice to resolve in Form 1 [Notice to Resolve a Family Law Matter],

**(b)** provide a copy of the notice to resolve to each other party,

**(c)** participate in a needs assessment under [rule 16 [participating in needs assessment]](rule_016_participating_in_needs_assessment.md),

**(d)** complete a parenting education program under [rule 17 [completing parenting education program]](rule_017_completing_parenting_education_program.md), and

**(e)** participate in at least one consensual dispute resolution session under [rule 18 [participating in consensual dispute resolution]](rule_018_participating_in_consensual_dispute_resolution.md).
