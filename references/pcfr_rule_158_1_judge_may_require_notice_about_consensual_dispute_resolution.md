# Rule 158.1 — Judge may require notice about consensual dispute resolution


If a judge has ordered or directed that a party participate in consensual dispute resolution with a family justice counsellor under [rule 56 [directions or orders to attend]](pcfr_rule_056_directions_or_orders_to_attend.md) or rule 108 (2) (e) (i) [what happens at family settlement conference], the judge may require the parties to obtain written notice from the family justice counsellor indicating the following:

**(a)** if it was determined that consensual dispute resolution was completed and, if so, the date of completion;

**(b)** if it was determined that consensual dispute resolution was not able to be accessed or was not appropriate;

**(c)** the kinds of family law matters that were addressed during consensual dispute resolution;

**(d)** the kinds of family law matters that are outstanding.


_Amendments: [en. B.C. Reg. 126/2021, Sch. 1, s. 13.]_
