# Rule 19 — Certain parties not required to comply with consensual dispute resolution


The requirements described in [rule 18 [participating in consensual dispute resolution]](pcfr_rule_018_participating_in_consensual_dispute_resolution.md) do not apply to any parties if one of the parties is

**(a)** the Director of Maintenance Enforcement, or

**(b)** a director under the Child, Family and Community Service Act.
