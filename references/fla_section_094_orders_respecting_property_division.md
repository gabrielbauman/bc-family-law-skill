# Section 94 — Orders respecting property division


**(1)** The Supreme Court may make an order under this Division on application by a spouse.


**(2)** The Supreme Court may not make an order respecting the division of property and family debt that is the subject of an agreement described in [section 93 (1) [setting aside agreements respecting property division]](fla_section_093_setting_aside_agreements_respecting_property_division.md), unless all or part of the agreement is set aside under that section.
