# Rule 49 — Completion of requirements


A judge or a family justice manager at a family management conference may make an order that a party complete the following, as applicable:

**(a)** the early resolution requirements described in [rule 10 [early resolution requirements must be met before application filed]](pcfr_rule_010_early_resolution_requirements_must_be_met_before_application_filed.md);

**(b)** Repealed. [B.C. Reg. 17/2026, s. 6.]

**(c)** the parenting education program registry requirements described in [rule 100 [requirements in parenting education program registries]](pcfr_rule_100_requirements_in_parenting_education_program_registries.md).
