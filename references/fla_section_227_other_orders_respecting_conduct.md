# Section 227 — Other orders respecting conduct


A court may make an order requiring a party to do one or more of the following:

**(a)** give security in any form the court directs;

**(b)** report to the court, or to a person named by the court, at the time and in the manner specified by the court;

**(c)** do or not do anything, as the court considers appropriate, in relation to a purpose referred to in [section 222 [purposes for which orders respecting conduct may be made]](fla_section_222_purposes_for_which_orders_respecting_conduct_may_be_made.md).
