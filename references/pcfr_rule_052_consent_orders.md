# Rule 52 — Consent orders


**(1)** Subject to subrule (2), a judge or a family justice manager at a family management conference may make orders with the consent of the parties, including final orders about one or more of the following:

**(a)** parental responsibilities;

**(b)** parenting time;

**(c)** child support;

**(d)** contact with a child;

**(e)** guardianship of a child;

**(f)** spousal support;

**(g)** property division in respect of a companion animal.


**(2)** A family justice manager at a family management conference may not make a final order about guardianship of a child under subrule (1).
