# Section 153 — Definitions


In this Division and the regulations made under [section 247 (2) [regulations respecting child support]](fla_section_247_regulations_respecting_child_support.md):


**"amended statement of recalculation"** means a statement of recalculation given after a correction is made under [section 156 [correction of recalculation]](fla_section_156_correction_of_recalculation.md);


**"child support service"** means the child support service established under [section 154 [establishment of child support service]](fla_section_154_establishment_of_child_support_service.md);


**"notification date"** means the date on which both the payor and the recipient are deemed, under the regulations, to have received a statement of recalculation or an amended statement of recalculation;


**"payor"** means a person who, under an agreement or order, must pay child support;


**"recalculated amount"** means the child support a payor must pay after recalculation under [section 155 [recalculation of child support]](fla_section_155_recalculation_of_child_support.md) by the child support service;


**"recipient"** means a person to whom child support must be paid;


**"statement of recalculation"** means a written notice by which the child support service notifies payors and recipients of recalculated amounts.
