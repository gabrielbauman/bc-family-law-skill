# Rule 155 — Deficient forms


**(1)** A clerk may refuse to accept a form for filing if it is not in the correct form or if the form is not completed in accordance with the instructions.


**(2)** A party may apply for an order under [rule 62 [case management orders — judge]](pcfr_rule_062_case_management_orders_judge.md) or 63 [case management orders — family justice manager] to permit the filing of a form that is deficient.
