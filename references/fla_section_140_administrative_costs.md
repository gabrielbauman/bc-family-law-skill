# Section 140 — Administrative costs


**(1)** If the administrator requires a fee to be paid to offset administrative costs incurred in dividing benefits under this Part,

**(a)** the fee may be no more than the prescribed amount, and

**(b)** a member and spouse are each responsible for paying the fee.


**(2)** Unless the parties agree otherwise, a member or spouse who pays more than a half share of a fee under subsection (1) may recover from the other the additional amount paid.


**(3)** An administrator must deduct a fee under subsection (1) from the payment of benefits unless the fee is paid by the member or the spouse, or both.
