# Rule 11 — Early resolution requirements must be met before reply filed


Before filing a reply under [rule 28 [reply to application about family law matter]](pcfr_rule_028_reply_to_application_about_family_law_matter.md) to an application about a family law matter in an early resolution registry, the party filing the reply must, unless otherwise provided in these rules,

**(a)** participate in a needs assessment under [rule 16 [participating in needs assessment]](pcfr_rule_016_participating_in_needs_assessment.md),

**(b)** complete a parenting education program under [rule 17 [completing parenting education program]](pcfr_rule_017_completing_parenting_education_program.md), and

**(c)** participate in at least one consensual dispute resolution session under [rule 18 [participating in consensual dispute resolution]](pcfr_rule_018_participating_in_consensual_dispute_resolution.md).
