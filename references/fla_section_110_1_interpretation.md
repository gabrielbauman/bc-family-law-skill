# Section 110.1 — Interpretation


In the following provisions, "benefit" includes a LIRA benefit or a LIF benefit:

**(a)** [section 111 (1) [benefits to be determined in accordance with this Part]](fla_section_111_benefits_to_be_determined_in_accordance_with_this_part.md);

**(b)** Division 2.1 [Division of Benefits Under Locked-in Retirement Accounts and Life Income Funds] of this Part;

**(c)** [section 125 [entitlement to survivor benefits]](fla_section_125_entitlement_to_survivor_benefits.md);

**(d)** [section 127 [agreements respecting division]](fla_section_127_agreements_respecting_division.md);

**(e)** [section 129 [reapportioning benefits]](fla_section_129_reapportioning_benefits.md);

**(f)** [section 130 [clarifying division of benefits]](fla_section_130_clarifying_division_of_benefits.md);

**(g)** [section 141 [income tax]](fla_section_141_income_tax.md);

**(h)** [section 144 [trust of survivor and pension benefits]](fla_section_144_trust_of_survivor_and_pension_benefits.md);

**(i)** [section 145 [no further entitlement after division of benefits]](fla_section_145_no_further_entitlement_after_division_of_benefits.md).
