# Rule 31 — If no reply filed


If a party does not file a reply within 30 days in accordance with [rule 28 [reply to application about a family law matter]](pcfr_rule_028_reply_to_application_about_family_law_matter.md),

**(a)** the party is not entitled to receive notice of any part of the proceedings, including any court appearance, and

**(b)** a judge or family justice manager may make orders in the absence of the party.
