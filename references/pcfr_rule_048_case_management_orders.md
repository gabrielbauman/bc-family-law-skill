# Rule 48 — Case management orders


**(1)** A judge at a family management conference may, based on information provided by or on behalf of the parties, make orders under [rule 62 [case management orders — judge]](pcfr_rule_062_case_management_orders_judge.md) to ensure that a file is ready to proceed to the next step in the process.


**(2)** A family justice manager at a family management conference may, based on information provided by or on behalf of the parties, make orders under [rule 63 [case management orders — family justice manager]](pcfr_rule_063_case_management_orders_family_justice_manager.md) to ensure that a file is ready to proceed to the next step in the process.
