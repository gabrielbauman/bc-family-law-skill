# Section 19.20 — Enforcement of arbitration awards


**(1)** Subject to this Act, if an arbitration award is filed in the court, the award is enforceable under this Act as if it were an order of the court.


**(2)** Despite any agreement of the parties to a family law dispute, a provision of an arbitration award that is inconsistent with this Act or the Divorce Act (Canada) is not enforceable.
