# Section 12 — Family justice counsellors not to be compelled


**(1)** Except as permitted under the regulations, the following persons must not be compelled to disclose, or to testify in any proceeding respecting, information obtained in the course of providing assistance under [section 10 (2) [family justice counsellors]](fla_section_010_family_justice_counsellors.md):

**(a)** a family justice counsellor;

**(b)** a person within a class of persons prescribed for the purposes of section 11 (1) (b) [confidentiality of information].


**(2)** For greater certainty, subsection (1) applies to the notes and records of the persons referred to in that subsection.
