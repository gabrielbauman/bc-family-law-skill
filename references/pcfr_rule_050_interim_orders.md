# Rule 50 — Interim orders


A judge or a family justice manager at a family management conference may make interim orders, including interim orders about one or more of the following:

**(a)** parental responsibilities;

**(b)** parenting time;

**(c)** child support;

**(d)** contact with a child;

**(e)** guardianship of a child;

**(f)** spousal support;

**(g)** property division in respect of a companion animal.
