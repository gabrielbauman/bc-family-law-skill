# Section 126 — Waiving division of benefits


**(1)** Before an administrator implements the division of benefits under a plan, a limited member or the personal representative of the limited member's estate may waive the division of benefits by giving notice in accordance with [section 136 [notice or waiver]](fla_section_136_notice_or_waiver.md).
