# Strategy

Private strategic material: counsel's guidance, assessments of the
other party, theories of the case, settlement thinking. **This folder
is not evidence and its content is never filed, served, quoted in
correspondence, or included in any document the other party or the
court will see.** It informs drafting decisions; it does not appear in
their output.

- Record lawyer guidance here (e.g. `lawyer-opinions.md`, one dated
  entry per opinion) and **consult it before revising any court-bound
  document** — counsel's selections control.
- Behavioural assessments of the other party are working hypotheses
  for preparation (what to expect in negotiation or cross), not facts;
  keep them professional in tone.
- If this case gains a lawyer later, this folder is the briefing file —
  another reason to keep it accurate and temperate.
