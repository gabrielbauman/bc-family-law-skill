# Section 56 — Limits on appointments


In making an appointment under [section 53 [appointment of guardian in case of death]](section_053_appointment_of_guardian_in_case_of_death.md) or 55 [appointment of standby guardian], the appointing guardian

**(a)** must consider the best interests of the child only, and

**(b)** may not grant greater parental responsibilities than the appointing guardian has with respect to the child.
