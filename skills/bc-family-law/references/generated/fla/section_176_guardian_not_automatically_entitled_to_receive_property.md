# Section 176 — Guardian not automatically entitled to receive property


Except as set out in [section 178 [delivery of small property]](section_178_delivery_of_small_property.md), a child's guardian is not, by reason only of being a guardian,

**(a)** a trustee of the child's property, or

**(b)** entitled to give a valid discharge on receiving property on behalf of the child.
