# Section 197 — Complying with duties respecting family dispute resolution


**(1)** If a lawyer is acting on behalf of a party in a proceeding under this Act, the lawyer must provide, at the time the proceeding is started, a statement, signed by the lawyer, certifying that the lawyer has complied with [section 8 (2) [duties of family dispute resolution professionals]](section_008_duties_of_family_dispute_resolution_professionals.md).


**(2)** A person making or intending to make an application under this Act must comply with any requirements set out in the regulations respecting mandatory family dispute resolution or prescribed procedures.
