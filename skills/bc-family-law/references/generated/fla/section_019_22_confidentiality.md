# Section 19.22 — Confidentiality


**(1)** Subject to this Act and any agreement of the parties, the parties and the arbitrator must not disclose proceedings, evidence, documents and information in connection with the arbitration that are not otherwise in the public domain.


**(2)** Subsection (1) does not apply if disclosure is

**(a)** required by law,

**(b)** required for the purposes of a proceeding under this Act, or

**(c)** authorized by a court order.
