# Rule 76 — Applying for orders about priority parenting matters


Subject to [rule 78 [priority parenting matters — without notice]](rule_078_priority_parenting_matters_without_notice.md), a party who is applying for, or applying to change a term or condition of or to cancel, an order about a priority parenting matter must file and serve on each other party

**(a)** the application for an order about a priority parenting matter in Form 15 [Application About Priority Parenting Matter], and

**(b)** any supporting evidence or documents.
