# Rule 110 — Trial readiness statement


Each party must file and serve a trial readiness statement in Form 22 [Trial Readiness Statement]

**(a)** at least 7 days before the date of the trial preparation conference if a trial preparation conference is scheduled, or

**(b)** as ordered by the court.


_Amendments: [am. B.C. Reg. 126/2021, Sch. 1, s. 10.]_
