# Rule 199 — Definitions


In this Division:


**"effective date of this Division"** means May 1, 2026;


**"family justice rules"** means rule 39 and every rule in Part 6, as those rules read immediately before the effective date of this Division;


**"former family justice registry"** means

**(a)** effective November 1, 2025, the Vancouver (Robson Square) registry,

**(b)** effective May 1, 2026, the Kelowna registry, and

**(c)** effective May 1, 2026, the Nanaimo registry.


_Amendments: [en. B.C. Reg. 17/2026, s. 11.]_
